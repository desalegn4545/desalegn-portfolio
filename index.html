Internship Project

“Networking Installation, Configuration, Maintenance and Troubleshooting at Ethio Telecom”[cite: 1]

The project addresses the gap between classroom networking theory and practical office operations by focusing on secure, structured LAN deployment, VLAN segmentation, and step-by-step troubleshooting.

1. Design & IP Addressing Plan

Divided private network 192.168.10.0/24 into four /26 subnets using VLSM/subnetting for Administration, Technical/IT, Customer Service, and Management VLANs[cite: 1].

2. Device Configuration

Configured Cisco switches and routers via CLI, creating access ports, 802.1Q trunk links, Inter-VLAN routing (Router-on-a-Stick), and DHCP pools[cite: 1].

3. Systematic Troubleshooting

Implemented a 6-step troubleshooting methodology following a bottom-up approach (OSI layers 1 through 7) to quickly isolate cable faults, mismatched masks, or gateway errors[cite: 1].
